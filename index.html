Kira is a personal, private content dashboard. Its creator, Marius Golz, uses it to prepare, schedule and publish his own GTA 6 gameplay clips to his own TikTok account.

How Kira uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the creator connects his TikTok account. Kira shows the connected display name and avatar.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish, video.upload): after the creator reviews a clip, writes a caption, picks the privacy level and confirms, Kira uploads the video to his TikTok profile or sends it to TikTok as a draft.

Kira never posts anything without an explicit confirmation by the creator. Kira is not a public service; no other users can sign up.

Kira is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or TikTok. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive.
